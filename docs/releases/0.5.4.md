# TURNTALE 0.5.4

- Landing feedback now uses three weights: ordinary singles move only the played tiles, combinations or any two shake the full view, and the final winning play receives a longer screen impact and a distinct haptic rhythm. The display's base rotation is preserved. Haptic settings and reduced-motion preferences still apply.
- Basic ivory tiles now integrate sun rays, moon crescents, star geometry, or cloud scrollwork into the number medallion, with a corresponding small suit mark. Twos have an extended engraved frame. Regional collectible tiles remain unchanged.
- Entrances restore the moonlit library and add resolution-independent scenery for the ten established challenge destinations. Actual opponents occupy one, two, or three world seats for two-, three-, and four-player games, with their saved names, portraits and public tile counts.
- Resume tiles have physical side walls. Double stacks show two layers without revealing a covered lower face or an opponent's hand.
- Entrance and live play share one round tabletop geometry and native-size material origin. Mirrored grain repeats avoid a hard texture seam across the large circle. The round surface remains under the controls while the interface fades in progressively; the tabletop no longer fades away to a rectangular surface.

Validation: typecheck, release rendering, impact precedence, saved-table privacy/rotation, world/camera projection and entrance continuity checks. Native 120 Hz rendering is retained; device refresh rate and vibration support remain browser/device dependent.
