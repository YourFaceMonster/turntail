## Portrait asset

`public/turntale-korean-portraits-058.webp`, generated with ImageGen and encoded to WebP without resizing or cropping. No third-party image package.

Prompt brief: one production-ready portrait atlas, 4 columns by 2 rows, equal square cells without gutters or borders; warm painterly storybook RPG portraits inspired by Joseon Korean villagers, centered heads and shoulders, consistent light and scale, no text, logos or watermarks. In reading order: elderly woman market vendor in wine-red hanbok; bearded middle-aged boatman in indigo and a headband; young male scholar with black gat and pale sage hanbok; young woman herbalist in green; young male ssireum wrestler in orange; middle-aged woman potter in blue; elderly male teacher with silver beard and plum hanbok; young woman musician in teal holding a flute. Distinct Korean faces, clothing and ages; no animals or Western costumes. Generated output was 1774×887 pixels and was visually inspected before use.
