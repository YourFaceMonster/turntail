# 0.5.10

- The main rail now uses the acting seat's persona accent, including the submitted-card review. The active character panel uses the same accent. Rally lighting no longer replaces the turn rail; the rally count remains.
- Restored the table's supports and character portraits during the seated opening. Supports start under the slab and end on the ground plane. Extra floor scenery remains omitted.
- Concealed opponent hands land in spaced rows on the tabletop, with responsive tile sizes for small screens. The opening uses low transit heights (at most 24 px above the required body/stack height), then keeps these backs visible through the framing step. They fade during the final overhead handoff rather than flying out of the viewport. Only the local hand is revealed.
- Intro and live table share a surface large enough to cover every viewport corner, including the conservative inset of organic stage silhouettes. The overhead view shows the table instead of the room background. Resume keeps the direct saved-board fade.
- Transparent speech bubbles have an outline and speaker name in the character's accent. Their left/right bounds follow the speaker's actual panel, update on resize, wrap long lines, and do not capture input.

Validation: TypeScript and production build; viewport coverage and opponent row separation for compact phones, portrait/landscape and desktop; low flight bounds and precise tabletop endpoints; support geometry, stage silhouettes and camera/body handoff; conversation privacy/idle regression and existing layout bounds. Browser checked the restored portraits/supports, full-table overhead screen, blue/coral turn accents, and transparent bubbles aligned to their character panels. The cloud browser uses the CSS fallback; physical-phone GPU frame rate is unmeasured.
