- Normal AI now uses three Korean folklore monsters: 청돌 (blue dokkaebi), 불찬 (ember dokkaebi), 돌해 (haetae). Full-body 3D-style rendered images replace the separate human face/body assembly. New games select unique cast members; restored normal games retain their hands, scores and table while updating legacy identities. Challenge casts remain independent.
- Drawing retains the current submitted combination until the lead actually returns. A confirmed new lead clears the submitted cards. Old saves cannot resurrect expired last-play tiles.
- Layered procedural tile audio replaces the repeated two-note action sounds. Five variations each cover selection, preparation, ordinary submission, heavy submission and drawing; multi-card plays add timed contacts, and drawing combines lift, brush and catch. Concealed/open draws use public event texture only. A shared noise buffer is reused, consecutive variants are avoided and finished audio nodes disconnect. Existing sound/music switches and haptics remain in control.

Validation: TypeScript and production build; shared camera zoom ratio and seams, post-overhead fade timing, flat body continuity, three-character roster uniqueness and saved-identity restoration, exact model/avatar source identity, lead-reset/save regression, sound-plan variety and audio-node lifecycle, conversation privacy and entrance/layout regression.
