# Turntale 0.5.13

- Repainted the three normal-mode monsters, room and walnut texture with a matching gouache/ink storybook treatment. Full-body and avatar use the same transparent illustration.
- Moved the character image planes beyond the rear rim. Removed the old vertical lift; the tabletop now masks their lower bodies naturally. Isolated the support render layer behind the tabletop to prevent legs bleeding through its surface.
- Louder, lower pitched tile impact, layered scrape/knock variations and bounded multi-tile gain. All Web Audio nodes still disconnect after playback.
- Four original instrumental loops: menu (50.53s), table (38.4s), victory (35.56s), reflection (56.47s). Deterministic source: scripts/compose-music.py. Local MP3 files, one unlocked audio element, volume ramps and background-tab pause.
- Four shake weights: single tile; pair/triple submission region; five-card combination or a two at table level; four-of-a-kind/straight-flush/round winner at full-scene level. XY displacement and small rotation. Reduced-motion preference suppresses shake.
- Smaller moon, larger star on normal tile corners. Other suits unchanged.
- Speech lives in the reserved left part of each status row; remaining-card count is aligned on the right with left-aligned contents. No speech overlay on the playing field.
- Bright gold current-journey badge and action; centered challenge opponents.
- Each opponent greets in sequence during the existing shuffle phase; no additional delay on resume.

Shared prompt: “Repaint in a coherent Korean fantasy storybook game art style: hand-painted gouache, gentle ink contours, simplified broad brush shapes, matte color, warm amber highlights and muted teal shadows. Keep original composition and proportions. Avoid photorealism, plastic 3D rendering, shiny pores, hard raytraced highlights, text or watermark.”

Subject constraints: preserve blue one-horn dokkaebi in indigo hanbok, red two-horn dokkaebi in yellow hanbok, ivory haetae with teal mane and gold bells; retain identities, poses and head placement, transparent full-body cutouts without ground shadows. Room keeps library/window/chairs and empty foreground; walnut remains a flat object-free wood-grain texture.
