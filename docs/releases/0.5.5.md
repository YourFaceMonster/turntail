# TURNTALE 0.5.5

- The existing result rows now show the round place beside each name and the accumulated place beside the held score. Rows sort by accumulated score, with equal totals sharing competition ranks (1st, 1st, 3rd). Invalid rounds do not claim a round place.
- Challenge results always show the carried starting balance, goal and remaining points. A clear previews the next region's target; bankruptcy clears, zero-point survival, overflow, loss and the final region use the unchanged progression rules.
- Each region clear has its own destination art, opponent portraits, newly unlocked tile/material set and next-location reveal. Ordinary round results stay in the existing result screen.
- Ten full-size cinematic environment plates replace the flat scenery. Region geometry now includes low mossy rock shelves, wet slate, basalt and obsidian altars, petrified wood, ancient roots and the final world-tree stump. Only actual wooden tables have furniture legs. Challenge surfaces follow their location; the selected collectible tile skin remains equipped. General games still use the selected full set.
- Basic tile faces and backs use photographed resin grain, quiet glaze, wear and bevel lighting while preserving the approved suit ornaments, bright yellow, selection dimensions and flat gameplay silhouettes. Resting and moving reverses share the same artwork.
- Intro tile thickness changes from about 38% of tile height to about 7.5%, shared with resume. Resume renders separate covered lower and upper bodies at their measured coordinates, using the same bevelled mesh and atlas as a new-game entrance. Hidden lower faces and AI hands never enter the render plan. WebGL loss falls back to lightweight two-piece CSS solids.
- Tile shadows project onto the stationary ground, with softer and wider shadows as tiles rise. Platform facets meet their top outline, directional light and contact shadows anchor the bases, and scene size leaves room for the environment around the play area. The permanent surface matches the entrance through UI handoff.

Validation: typecheck; result/tie ranking; challenge carried/remaining scores and all clear states; native asset presence; 10 solid platform geometries; resume face/back orientation, layer positions and privacy; existing progression/save tests; 18 physical deal scenes and 12 shuffle scenes; camera/frame alignment and collectible rendering. Device refresh rate and subjective atmosphere remain dependent on the browser and display.

Assets: the built-in image generation tool produced 10 location backgrounds plus ivory and stone material photographs. Prompts and exact project paths are recorded in [generation manifest](../assets/0.5.5-generation.json). All referenced assets are bundled under `public/`, with no external image service or full-resolution per-frame image work.
